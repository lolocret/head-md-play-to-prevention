# Echo

Lorie CRETTEX
Nouria DA ROS

## Pitch

The player embodies a student in a class that welcomes Echo, a hologram child. Through the scenes, their choices make them a witness, an accomplice or an ally facing the cyberbullying Echo suffers.

## Keywords

Identity, body, boundaries, nomophobia.

## Universe

**Holo’Kids program, coming soon to your class.** Echo is the first hologram child of the Holo’Kids program, developed by Altaya, an experimental research institute on new technologies. Holo’Kids are integrated into schools as teaching tools: they support students in difficulty and strengthen solidarity within the class.

**Echo’s emotional system.** Echo expresses their emotions through flowers that open or close. This visual signal lets the player read what Echo feels without Echo having to say it.

**Core mechanic.** Each choice quietly changes the bond between the player and Echo. A strong enough bond unlocks access to Echo’s story (their lore) later in the game. Without getting closer, that part of the story stays closed.

## Walkthrough

**Scene 1: The arrival.** During a talk on diversity, the teacher M. Kovasko introduces Echo to the class for the first time.
- First impression: curiosity, surprise or unease (“weird”).
- Attitude: look at Echo with curiosity or with disdain.

**Scene 2: The first “Hello”.** Echo greets the class and nobody answers. He reaches out to a student, but his program glitches and his hand passes through. An awkward moment, laughter in the class. Under stress, his flowers open clumsily. A student films the scene and posts the video in the class WhatsApp group, with “You’re not real” and “You’re a fake”.

**Scene 3: The Bouquet.** “The Bouquet” is the class’s private group, which Echo is excluded from. A meme mocking Echo circulates there.
- Like the post.
- Write a comment.
- Don’t react and watch.

**Scene 4: The next day.** The mockery of Echo goes on in class.
- Tell the teacher.
- Say nothing.
- Laugh along.

**Scene 5: The hack.** A student hacks Echo’s system to send it mocking digital stickers.
- Step in to stop it.
- Watch without acting.
- Make a sticker too.

## Endings

A harassment gauge tracks how much the player harasses Echo.

- **If you harass:** go too far and Echo short-circuits and starts harassing the harassers.
- **If you stay passive:** the plan to turn Echo off goes ahead.
- **If you help Echo:** the plan doesn’t happen. You go through Echo’s family story with them, and things get better in class: no more bullying, no to cyberbullying.
