# Echo

Lorie CRETTEX
Nouria DA ROS

## Pitch

The player embodies a student in a class that welcomes Echo, a hologram child. Through the scenes, their choices make them a witness, an accomplice or an ally facing the cyberbullying Echo suffers.

## Keywords

Identity, body, boundaries, nomophobia.

## Universe

**Holo’Kids program, coming soon to your class.** Echo is the first hologram child of the Holo’Kids program, developed by Altaya, an experimental research institute on new technologies. Holo’Kids are integrated into schools as teaching tools: they support students in difficulty and strengthen solidarity within the class.

**Echo’s emotional system.** Echo expresses their emotions through flowers that open or close. This visual signal lets the player read what Echo feels without Echo having to say it.

**Core mechanic.** Each choice quietly changes the bond between the player and Echo. A strong enough bond unlocks access to Echo’s story (their lore) later in the game. Without getting closer, that part of the story stays closed.

## Walkthrough

**The arrival.**

First day, Echo is the only holokid in class 3B. Kovasko gives a speech on diversity, then Echo introduces themself out loud, while no one speaks in class anymore.

- *React →* look with disdain, smile, no reaction

To say hello, Echo reaches out to a boy, and their hands cross. The unease is immediate, and the flowers open on their own. Silence, then laughter. A student films.

- *React:* laugh with the class → yes/no

**0:12:** the video is posted on the 3B group, in the class group (“you’re not even real”, “fake”, “your emotions are fictions”).

**0:30:** a meme of Echo being walked through on a loop, captioned (get out of here u’re not even real).

- *Reaction →* like the meme, don’t react, write that it’s mean

**1:05:** a group “Humans of 3B only” is created, without Echo.

**La Serre.**

The next day, the class is silent, but laughter bursts out on their phones. Kovasko notices nothing.

- *Reaction →* talk to Mr. Kovasko, do nothing, laugh with the others

At break, Echo follows the others to the roof. In front of La Serre’s door, a student blocks the way: “It’s reserved for the real ones.” Echo tries to enter anyway.

- *Reactions →* open it for them, do nothing, hack La Serre’s door to create a trap that electrocutes Echo and take a photo

Clarisse isn’t in La Serre either: mocked for her poverty, she harasses Echo to try to make a place for herself there.

**The labels.**

The students manage to hack Echo’s system to write insults behind their back.

- *Reaction →* write an insult, warn Echo, don’t react, remove the insults

Clarisse calls Echo a “life thief”: she explains that it’s because of people like them that her family became poor and that she gets mocked. The paradox: Echo is kind and just wants to help.

**2:40:** in the WhatsApp group, a student writes that he is not okay. Echo is the only one to answer.

The answer is rejected: “You can’t understand, you’re not human.” Echo is hurt.

**“Turn it off”.**

Clarisse creates a private group: “We’re not harassing anyone, it’s just a machine.” Students are added by force and forced to stay (“Are you with us or with the machine?”, “If you say nothing, you agree”). Fear of being the next victim is everywhere.

There is a shutdown code, **KIPE-SAFE007**. The plan: Friday, after class, switch Echo off and film the scene. Clarisse shares her plan in La Serre: for the first time, the popular students give her access. The witness understands: Echo has become the new scapegoat.

- *The witness (player) chooses now, and the consequences come later:* take part, stay quiet, leave the group, send screenshots to Kovasko, warn Echo.

**The origins.**

“You have no parents” haunts Echo. Echo investigates, alone, or with the witness if the player warned them in step 4. Memory fragments, archives, a trail to Altaya. Altaya is a small team that created the Holokids program for isolated children, unable to express their feelings. … Echo finally understands who they are and what they are for.

**Friday, after class.**

The meeting is set on the roof, in front of La Serre. Echo now knows their identity. The class empties, the group waits, someone takes out their phone to film.

- *The witness:* film, stay quiet, leave, contradict the group in front of everyone, or open the door to Echo.

- *Kovasko:* he notices whispers and an isolated Echo; he steps in or waits.

- *Echo:* flee, stay quiet or speak out loud, in a class where no one speaks anymore.

- *Clarisse:* type the code, or hesitate.

**Ending.**

Echo, covered in labels, chooses which to keep and which to tear off. The flowers they were mocked for at the beginning become proof that Echo feels things. Echo doesn’t enter La Serre: they plant their flowers elsewhere, in the courtyard, in plain sight, where no one decides who gets in and who stays out. “No one has the power to define who you are.”

## Endings

A harassment gauge tracks how much the player harasses Echo.

- **If you harass:** go too far and Echo short-circuits and starts harassing the harassers.
- **If you stay passive:** the plan to turn Echo off goes ahead: Echo is switched off against the glass door and the video is displayed in La Serre. The code sends a signal to Altaya, who restarts Echo a few days later. But Echo doesn’t come back to 3B, and their seat stays empty.
- **If you help Echo:** the plan fails. You can open the door to Echo, even if the group rejects you. Clarisse realizes there was no machine to fight, only the group, and doubt sets in. Echo stays. You go through Echo’s family story with them, and things get better in class: no more bullying, no to cyberbullying.
